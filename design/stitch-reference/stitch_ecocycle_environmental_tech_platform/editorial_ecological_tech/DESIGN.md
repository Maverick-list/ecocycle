## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an editorial, high-trust civic tech sensibility that positions environmental infrastructure as high-value, modern, and precise. It rejects the informal "craft-paper and watercolor" tropes of conventional eco-branding in favor of architectural authority, calculated asymmetry, and premium sustainability metrics.

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Editorial Architectural Precision:** Structured grids, refined proportional contrasts, and editorial typography that convey institutional rigor alongside modern technological elegance.
- **Organic Modernism:** A calibrated synthesis of organic deep tones (Forest Green, Sage, Warm Ivory) energized by a single high-visibility catalyst (Fresh Lime) used strictly for critical data moments, status triggers, and active conversions.
- **Civic Dignity & Transparency:** Data dashboards, tracking manifests, and impact summaries feel definitive and institutional, inspiring confidence across civic leaders, corporate enterprises, and citizen collectors.

### Tone & Voice (Bahasa Indonesia)
- **Authoritative yet Engaging:** Direct, purposeful, and dignified (e.g., *"Ubah Sampah Menjadi Nilai"*, *"Neraca Siklus Material"*, *"Jejak Pemulihan Sirkular"*).
- **Metric-Driven:** Prioritizes verified quantities (kg, ton, metrik reduksi karbon) over ambiguous eco-platitudes.

## Colors

The palette is constructed around deep arboreal foundations punctuated by luminous bio-luminescent accents.

### Color Architecture
- **Primary Canvas & Backgrounds:** The base substrate is Warm Ivory (`#F7F7F0`), not stark sterile white. Surface layers stack from Warm Ivory to Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, with Soft Sage (`#E5EDE2`) functioning as structural grounding for sub-sections and data trays.
- **Deep Anchors:** Primary Forest Green (`#123B2A`) and Deep Forest (`#0B261D`) govern authoritative typography, navigation anchors, hero banners, and high-impact containers.
- **Active Middle Tier:** Emerald (`#16845B`) serves as the active UI state color, interactive link tone, progress indicator, and validation state.
- **The Kinetic Catalyst:** Fresh Lime (`#C8ED72`) is high-contrast and luminous. It is applied exclusively to critical CTAs on dark containers, focal pill badges, micro-metric callouts, and live tracking pings. It is never used for large background areas or body text.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Dark Text (`#202A24`) for primary editorial headings and dense data readouts. Secondary Text (`#6B766E`) for metadata, contextual labels, timestamps, and architectural borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the architectural geometric distinction of Plus Jakarta Sans for titles and editorial framing with the neutral technical clarity of Manrope for dense data, body copy, and UI controls.

### Typographic Principles
- **Title Tracking & Proportions:** Headlines feature tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver an authoritative, publication-style density.
- **Micro-Labels & Metadata:** All operational markers, status cues, and category flags use `label-caps` in uppercase format with wide tracking (`0.08em`) to enforce legibility alongside numeric values.
- **Numerical Staging:** Key metrics utilize `metric-display` with tabular numbers enabled (`tnum`), paired with adjacent unit labels (`label-caps`) aligned to baseline or baseline offset.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetric 12-column editorial bento grid, favoring varied span groupings (e.g., 7+5 column spreads, 8+4 analytical splits, and 4+4+4 module arrays) over repetitive uniform cards.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column fluid grid, `margin`: `3rem`, `gutter`: `1.5rem`. Max container bound of `1380px` centered.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin`: `2rem`, `gutter`: `1rem`. Bento modules reflow to equalized double columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin-sm`: `1.25rem`, `gutter-sm`: `1rem`. Asymmetric desktop modules fold into a clean vertical stack with unified edge padding.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Internal Padding:** Small badges and list items utilize `space-xs` and `space-sm`. Standard input containers, cards, and modal components rely on `space-md` to `space-lg`.
- **Section Isolation:** Distinct analytical modules and editorial blocks are segregated by `space-xl` to maintain breathable white space against the Warm Ivory substrate.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy drop shadows and fuzzy ambient spreads, opting instead for architectural tonal layering paired with razor-thin hairline borders.

### Elevation Strategy
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Warm Ivory (`#F7F7F0`), non-elevated.
- **Layer 1 (Standard Bento Card):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surface framed by a 1px solid border in `#202A24` at 8% opacity (`rgba(32, 42, 36, 0.08)`). No shadow in resting state.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Active/Hover):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) supported by a crisp 1px border in `#123B2A` at 18% opacity, backed by an ultra-subtle tinted shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 38, 29, 0.06)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Overlays & Floats):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Deep Forest (`#0B261D`) bounded by 1px hairline border at 15% opacity with an ambient base: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(11, 38, 29, 0.14)`.
- **Dark Surface Layering:** On Deep Forest cards (`#0B261D`), secondary child panels utilize Forest Green (`#123B2A`) with hairline borders tinted in Soft Sage at 12% opacity (`rgba(229, 237, 226, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The interface balances crisp architectural discipline with ergonomic touch targets through unified, deliberate border radiuses.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Containers & Bento Cards:** `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) establishes a calm, modern profile without appearing balloon-like.
- **Inner Sub-Elements & Inputs:** Inputs, dropdown panels, and inner data buckets use standard `rounded` (8px / 0.5rem).
- **Indicators & Micro-Badges:** Pill badges, dynamic tags, and interactive action chips employ full pill styling (`9999px`) to immediately signal interactive or status properties.
- **Interactive Form Elements:** Checkboxes adopt a minimal 4px radius; radio toggles are strict circles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action):** Background Deep Forest (`#0B261D`), typography in Warm Ivory (`#F7F7F0`), padding `12px 24px`, height `48px`, shape `rounded` (8px). On dark containers, Primary Button switches to Fresh Lime (`#C8ED72`) fill with Deep Forest (`#0B261D`) typography.
- **Secondary (Outlined):** Transparent fill, 1px border in `#123B2A` (20% opacity), text in Primary Forest Green (`#123B2A`). Hover transitions to Soft Sage (`#E5EDE2`) fill at 50% opacity.
- **Tertiary (Ghost):** Zero border, text in Emerald (`#16845B`), paired with directional arrows (`→`). Subtle underline on hover.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Status Indicator Badges:** Full-pill silhouette (`9999px`), padding `4px 12px`, type scale `label-caps`. 
  - *Active / Optimal:* Soft Sage background (`#E5EDE2`) with Primary Forest Green text (`#123B2A`) and a pulsing 6px dot in Fresh Lime (`#C8ED72`).
  - *Pending / Logistics:* Warm Ivory (`#F7F7F0`) with 1px border in `#6B766E` (20% opacity) and Dark Text.
- **Filter Chips:** Height `36px`, rounded pill (`9999px`), border 1px solid in `#202A24` at 10% opacity. Selected state: background in Primary Forest Green (`#123B2A`), text in Warm Ivory (`#F7F7F0`).

### Cards & Bento Modules
- **Metric Cards:** White (`#FFFFFF`) ground with hairline structural border. Top anchor contains metric category in `label-caps` (`#6B766E`), center displays numerical value in `metric-display` (`#202A24`), bottom anchor shows trend percentage pill badge.
- **Impact Showcase Bento:** Deep Forest (`#0B261D`) hero module. Headlines rendered in Warm Ivory (`#F7F7F0`). Callout metrics and live updates surfaced in Fresh Lime (`#C8ED72`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Fields:** Height `48px`, background Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), border 1px solid `rgba(32, 42, 36, 0.15)`, radius `8px`. Focus state shifts border to Emerald (`#16845B`) with an ambient 2px glow in `#16845B` at 15% opacity. Floating label in `label-md`.
- **Selection Controls:** Checkboxes and radios use a 1.5px border in Primary Forest Green (`#123B2A`). Checked state fills with Primary Forest Green, featuring a crisp check icon in Fresh Lime (`#C8ED72`).

### Data Visualizations & Progress Trackers
- **Sirkular Bar Trackers:** Track base in Soft Sage (`#E5EDE2`), height `8px`, radius `9999px`. Progress fill in Emerald (`#16845B`) or dual gradient into Fresh Lime (`#C8ED72`).
- **Data Tables:** Clean horizontal hairline dividers (no vertical lines). Header row set in `label-caps` with Secondary Text color (`#6B766E`). Numeric data cells right-aligned in tabular font format.
